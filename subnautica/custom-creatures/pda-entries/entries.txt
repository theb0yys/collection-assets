Acid Spider

Lifeforms / Fauna

A large pursuit predator of Mountain Island, with long jointed legs and a rounded, bristled abdomen.

1. Ground Movement:
The legs spread widely around the body as it crosses the terrain. Once it begins an approach, the area occupied by the animal becomes much more apparent than it was at rest.

2. Pursuit:
Follows intruders over the ground within its territory and attacks on contact. Its willingness to close the distance makes narrow island paths particularly poor places to stop. Reaching dry land has removed one survival problem and introduced another.

Assessment: Hostile land predator - Leave its territory

A Predator Fang can be recovered when a specimen dies nearby. Combine two fangs with one Glass to fabricate Enameled Glass.

Acid Worm

Lifeforms / Fauna

A large segmented predator which crawls across the Grand Reef seafloor. Its length is approximately 5.5m, much of which can be difficult to separate from the surrounding rock and vegetation.

1. Body Segments:
The dark body joins a broad, heavily textured rear section to a more articulated head. Individual sections can be mistaken for separate features of the seabed until their movement reveals one continuous animal.

2. Feeding Approach:
Remains within a small area and closes on nearby prey along the bottom. A diver descending to examine the terrain can enter its reach before recognizing the full outline.

Assessment: Ambush hazard - Maintain clearance above occupied ground

Kelp Piranha

Lifeforms / Fauna

A small predator which hunts in coordinated schools among the creepvines. Individual specimens measure approximately 0.75m, with a round body, red eyes and exposed teeth.

1. Shared Pursuit:
Members of a school concentrate on the same prey. Bite attempts are staggered, so the withdrawal of one fish may be followed by the approach of another. Tracking the nearest mouth is an incomplete assessment of the situation.

2. Disruption:
A larger predator can scatter the school and interrupt its hunt. Members also pause when prey is lost or killed before resuming their activity. Their coordination does not prevent them from becoming prey themselves.

Assessment: Avoid hunting schools - Expect successive approaches

Megalodon

Lifeforms / Fauna / Leviathans

A fifteen-meter predatory leviathan found in the Dunes. Pale, beadlike markings run along the flanks of its heavy blue-gray body.

1. Dentition:
Broad jaws carry prominent serrated teeth. The animal closes on its target before delivering a bite, then briefly breaks off the attack. This interval should be used to leave.

2. Predation:
Pursues smaller animals through a substantial territory, including predators that would themselves threaten a diver. A hammerhead is small enough to attract its attention. Occupying a respectable position in the food chain offers limited protection here.

Assessment: Severe threat - avoid its hunting territory

A nearby death can yield one Megalodon Tooth and one Leviathan Cartilage sample. Acquiring the tooth unlocks the Megalodon Tooth Knife blueprint.
